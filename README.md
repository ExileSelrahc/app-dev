# Jujutsu Kaisen (呪術廻戦)

> *"Sorcery Battle"*

**Jujutsu Kaisen** (*呪術廻戦*, lit. **"Sorcery Battle"**) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by **Gege Akutami**.

- **Published by:** Shueisha  
- **Magazine:** Weekly Shōnen Jump  
- **Serialization:** March 2018 — September 2024  
- **Volumes:** 30 tankōbon volumes  

## Story Overview

The story follows high school student **Yuji Itadori**, who becomes the host of the powerful Curse **Ryomen Sukuna** after joining a secret organization of **Jujutsu Sorcerers**. Together, they fight against malevolent supernatural forces known as *Curses*.
